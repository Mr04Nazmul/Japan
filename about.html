About myjapan

myjapan is an independent personal project by Hossain Najmul. It helps travellers record visited Japanese prefectures and create a personal travel poster.

The map, searchable list and export preview work together: mark a place, see your progress and save a PNG or SVG keepsake. The site is a personal record tool, not an official tourism service, booking service or navigation map.

How we describe progress

Each prefecture counts once. The displayed percentage is the number of selected prefectures divided by 47. You decide what counts as a visit. The app does not verify your location or claim that a coloured prefecture means every part has been explored.

Contact and corrections

For bugs, accessibility problems or corrections, open an issue on the project repository. Include the page, device/browser and steps needed to reproduce the problem. This is a public forum; do not include passwords, your address or private travel details.

Your information

Your name and map selections stay in your browser's local storage. Export sharing is handled by the destination you choose. Read the privacy page for details about hosting and third-party services.
