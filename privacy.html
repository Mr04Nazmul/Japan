Privacy

myjapan lets you mark Japanese prefectures you have visited and generate a personal travel poster.

Your travel data

Your entered name and visited prefectures are saved in this browser's local storage. myjapan does not upload these fields to its own server. Clear the map or browser storage to remove them. Exported pictures are generated on your device; when you share a picture, the destination you choose receives it.

Advertising and hosting

The site is submitted to Google AdSense, but ad loading and ad units are currently disabled on the interactive map page. If advertising is enabled later, the following information applies. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to serve, measure and personalize advertising, subject to your location, choices and applicable requirements. Ad availability and approval are controlled by Google. Read Google's advertising technology information and manage preferences in My Ad Center. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement. Google Fonts requests may disclose technical connection information to Google.

Map and QR

The progress percentage is the number of visited prefectures divided by 47; it does not measure land area. The QR code links to the myjapan homepage.
